Tesla internship check

Tesla's site only serves real browsers, so your watcher can't fetch it. This bookmark does the check inside your browser when you click it, as part of a normal visit.

  1. Show your bookmarks bar (Chrome: ⌘⇧B on Mac, Ctrl+Shift+B on Windows).
  2. Drag this button onto the bookmarks bar: Tesla check
  3. Open tesla.com/careers and click Tesla check.

A panel lists Tesla internships that match your config.json watches and aren't open on Simplify, with estimated posting dates. NEW marks roles this browser hasn't shown you before.
